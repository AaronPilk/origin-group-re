Capabilities

Every Property Has An Origin.

Origin Group executes structured real estate investments grounded in disciplined strategy, asset advisory, and long-term performance.

01 / 04

Transaction Advisory & Execution

We structure and execute acquisitions, recapitalizations, repositionings, and dispositions across a range of commercial asset types. Each transaction is grounded in underwriting discipline, strategic positioning, and clear alignment of objectives from initial analysis through closing.

  • Investment Sales and LeasingAcross Retail, Restaurant, Medical, Office, Industrial, Land
  • Landlord and Tenant Advisory
  • Owner-User Transactions
  • Confidential and Off-Market Processes

02 / 04

Asset-Level & Portfolio Advisory

We oversee asset strategy across operational, leasing, and capital planning phases to drive performance and long-term value. Our approach integrates repositioning strategy, tenant curation, and disciplined asset management.

Advisory assignments may include:

  • Hold vs. Sell Analysis
  • Asset Repositioning Strategy
  • Portfolio Planning and Sequencing
  • Long-Term Real Estate Planning

03 / 04

Owner-User & Operating Company Advisory

We work closely with business owners and operating companies where real estate decisions directly impact operations, growth, and capital allocation.

Our work considers both real estate and business objectives, ensuring ownership, leasing, or monetization decisions support the broader enterprise.

Services may include:

  • Owner-User Transactions
  • Sale-Leaseback Advisory
  • Expansion, Consolidation, and Relocation Planning
  • Coordination with Lenders, Attorneys, Wealth Managers, Estate Planners, and Professional Advisors

04 / 04

Market Intelligence & Underwriting

We provide valuation insight and market analysis to support informed decision-making.

Investment decisions are informed by active market knowledge, transaction experience, and rigorous underwriting. Emphasis is placed on clarity of basis, timing, and risk-adjusted execution.

Services include:

  • Pricing and Valuation Guidance
  • Market and Submarket Analysis
  • Underwriting and Feasibility Support
  • Strategic Review of Alternatives
